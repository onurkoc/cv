Profile

More than 18 years of experience in tunnelling and underground construction, with projects in Europe, North and South America, the Middle East and North Africa, including the Semmering Base Tunnel, the Eurasia Tunnel in Istanbul, the Granitztal tunnel chain of the Koralm Railway and hydropower projects in Israel and Chile. Currently leads a team of five engineers and carries out independent checking of structural designs for SBB infrastructure projects.

Key skills

  • Design of complex underground structures
  • Underground works for hydropower: steel-lined pressure shaft, pressure tunnels, surge tank
  • Independent checking of structural designs (Category 3 check)
  • NATM, TBM, cut-and-cover
